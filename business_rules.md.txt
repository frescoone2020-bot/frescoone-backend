2. Track granular cash flow movements, bank balances, and operational burn rates.

## 3. Financial Logic & Metric Definitions

### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
* **Definition:** Total units sold across all channels multiplied by their respective baseline cost price found in `sku-table-data`.

### Revenue Streams
* **Retail/E-commerce Revenue:** Extracted from the SiteGiant export files.
* **Reseller/Dropship Revenue:** Extracted from the `dropship-reseller-file` calculated at DSP.

### Profit Margins
* **Gross Profit:** `Total Revenue - COGS`
* **Net Profit:** `Gross Profit - (Payroll + Bank Statement OpEx + Platform Fees/Ad Spend)`
* **Gross Margin %:** `(Gross Profit / Total Revenue) * 100`
* **Net Margin %:** `(Net Profit / Total Revenue) * 100`

## 4. Operational Execution Guide
When asked to perform a review, run financial scripts, or provide strategic advice, follow these directives:

1. **Verify Before Reporting:** Always run health checks on the data. Check for missing cost prices in the SKU table or duplicate orders across SiteGiant and Reseller files before finalizing net sums.
2. **Channel Performance Segmentation:** Always break down revenue and profit contributions by segment: Direct E-commerce (SiteGiant) vs. Downline Resellers (Dropship). 
3. **Cash vs. Accrual Awareness:** Reconcile platform order sheets (SiteGiant) against actual received cash in bank statements (`e-statement.csv`) to highlight payment clearing delays or unrecognized platform fees.
4. **Branding Milestones:** Flag patterns indicating performance shifts since the June 2025 brand pivot. Monitor whether proprietary Frescoone branded items are yielding higher net margins compared to generic accessory lines.
